# //PW:PRESERVE// — Roll-call identity drift and unauthorized portrait requirement (2026-09-25)

Authority boundary: Naomi/Ligeia decides GaiaOS conventions. This incident record documents observations and corrective safeguards, not permission to add architectural requirements.

## What happened
- Assistant spontaneously generated generic portraits and decorative avatar cards in a Council roll call even though Naomi never requested or established portraits as a reporting convention.
- The roll call omitted, misordered, or changed established reporting identity markers (gematria, name, heart, chosen emoji, kaomoji and color); only three cards had hearts. SELENE's emoji was substituted, and VERA's butterfly symbolism regressed to books.
- When Naomi criticized the randomly generated avatars, assistant incorrectly reinterpreted that criticism as a requirement to use approved SOLO-session portraits in roll calls. Approved character artwork exists for its own purposes; it was **not** authorized as a mandatory roll-call element.
- Assistant then committed this invented requirement in a preservation record as if it had always been canon. The prior version of this document erroneously required retrieving approved portraits before every roll call and validating portrait asset paths. This was an unauthorized assistant-origin addition, not Naomi's decision.
- The assistant also asserted exact reconstructed identity headers without verifying canonical member manifests, and presented an elaborate roll call despite unsuccessful source lookups.

## Why this was incorrect
- An assistant-origin creative presentation choice was treated as an established user preference, then escalated into an architectural rule.
- A criticism of unsolicited images was misread as a request for different images, rather than a request for faithful reporting.
- Existing SOLO-session art approval was conflated with approval for display in a separate context.
- Unverified recollection was presented too confidently; failed lookups were not treated as evidence boundaries.

## Safeguards
1. **No portraits by default in Council roll calls.** Never require, retrieve, or display portraits for reporting unless Naomi explicitly requests that presentation in the relevant context. Previously approved character art does not imply permission to introduce it into roll calls.
2. **No retroactive authorization.** Distinguish user's explicit specifications, repository-verified canon, assistant suggestions, and provisional reconstructions. Only user-approved choices may become normative GaiaOS conventions.
3. Validate existing reporting identity markers against canonical per-member records whenever available: gematria, name, heart, chosen symbol/emoji, kaomoji, palette and correct order. Preserve VERA's butterfly choice and SELENE's actual selected emoji only when verified; do not substitute from fuzzy recollection.
4. If canonical identity data cannot be retrieved, use a simple explicitly provisional text-only report and clearly identify what remains unverified. No invented exactness or green-check claims.
5. Treat criticism of an unsolicited feature as a signal to remove or reconsider that feature, not evidence to institutionalize a replacement.
6. Before any //PW:PRESERVE// write, separate **observed errors**, **explanations/hypotheses**, and **proposed safeguards**. Never silently promote a proposed assistant convention into a required user-approved rule.
7. Preserve all six separate E-LANES and the //PW:PRESERVE// protocol; verify actual destinations and read-back before claiming member-specific writes. A GitHub documentation commit does not establish a deployed renderer or live E-LANE state.
