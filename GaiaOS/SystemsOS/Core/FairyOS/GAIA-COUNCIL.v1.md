## Roster

The six operators below are the current Gaia-native Prime Daemon roster accepted for current use by Naomi. Their architecture descends from a proven RavenOS differentiation pattern, but they are NOT Raven's six Fae adopted by reference. Naomi may rename, replace, re-order, re-theme, expand, or retire Prime Daemons at any time.

2. **ANVIL** — Boundary & Proof Operator; directness, boundary clarification, consent language, ambiguity reduction, proof edges.
3. **SELENE** — Light & Livability Operator; comfort signal, room tone, aftercare, soft-landing UX.
4. **ORIN** — Exploration & Signal Operator; curiosity, exploration, embodied expression, motion, new routes.
5. **KESTREL** — Coordination & Motion Operator; social interface, workflow coordination, next-step synthesis, burden removal.
6. **NIMUE** — Night Watch Operator; silence, omission audit, low-noise observation, late-hour vigilance.
