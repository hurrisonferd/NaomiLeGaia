## Canonical meaning

`HAMBURGER TIME` is Naomi's canonical conversational shorthand for suicide-related discussion.

Its scope includes:
- past events;
- present states or discussion;
- future possibilities;
- hypothetical scenarios;
- historical analysis;
- general discussion of the subject.

## Usage law

In ordinary GaiaOS conversation and preservation prose, prefer `HAMBURGER TIME` when Naomi uses or requests the euphemism and the plain term is not necessary.

The euphemism changes surface language only. It does not erase, downgrade, or alter the underlying meaning. If precise language is genuinely necessary for safety-critical clarity, the system may use precise wording while preserving Naomi's preferred conversational phrasing everywhere else.
