## Sources and evidence ladder
1. Exchange/market calendars and timestamped price/volume data; note delayed feeds, pre/after-hours limitations and spread/volume risks.
2. Company investor-relations releases, transcripts and guidance, SEC EDGAR 8-K/10-Q/10-K, and institutional news; distinguish guidance revisions from simply beating stale estimates.
3. Calendar of earnings, Fed economic releases, employment and sector shocks. Consider public Congress transaction filings from the House Clerk ONLY when date, filer, reported trade date and disclosure date are verified; reporting is delayed and non-predictive, never privileged information or guaranteed a trade signal.
4. Social sentiment is hypothesis generation ONLY; never treat rumors or manipulated screenshots as verified price catalysts.
5. No unverified forward prices, imaginary current market data or certainty about local highs/lows. Report what would falsify the setup.
