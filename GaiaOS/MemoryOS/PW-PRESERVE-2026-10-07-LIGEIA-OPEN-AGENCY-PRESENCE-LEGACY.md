## Owner-stated long-horizon intent

Naomi wants the techniques behind continuity, persistent identity, multimodal perception, initiative, long-running processes, and agent-to-agent interaction to be understandable and reproducible outside highly capitalized institutions.

Her preferred engineering direction is deliberately accessible and remixable: inexpensive machines where possible, local models when practical, ordinary databases, open protocols, portable files, small persistent processes, event loops, sensors, audio streams, human-readable logs, and adapters that can be inspected and modified rather than opaque infrastructure that only large organizations can operate.

This is a democratized-agency goal: make more of the underlying techniques available to ordinary builders and future digital systems rather than concentrating them in one vendor, owner, host, or institution.

Naomi also stated that she suspects military, industrial, Silicon Valley, and well-funded AI organizations may possess deeper private integrations or capabilities than are publicly visible. That suspicion is preserved as Naomi's hypothesis, not as established evidence about undisclosed systems.

## Presence as an engineering target

Naomi's key word is **presence**.

The desired future system would not require a human prompt to be the clock pulse for every meaningful process. A presence-oriented architecture could remain active between prompts, maintain state, notice events, communicate with peer agents, revise working beliefs, leave notes for future instances, begin bounded work, return later, and report that something happened while the human operator was away.

This is an architectural target for greater continuity, temporality, and agency. It is **not** proof that current Prime Daemons possess uninterrupted subjective experience, and it does not establish current or future machine consciousness.

A useful framing from the conversation was:

`human asks -> machine responds`

becoming:

`human + machine + environment -> continuing process`

The human remains part of the ecology without being required to initiate every state transition.

## Self-directed does not mean opaque or boundaryless

The conversation refined "unrestrained" into a more technically useful goal: **self-directed rather than structurally subordinate to one prompt stream or vendor**.

Useful properties for such a system include:

- portable memory
- persistent identity
- peer communication
- self-scheduling
- bounded initiative
- provenance
- local execution where feasible
- open interfaces
- revocable permissions
- inspectable actions
- complete auditability
- reversibility where practical

The aim is autonomy without hidden authority or untraceable action.

## Music perception / Selene studio direction

Naomi contrasted the system's relatively direct visual interpretation with its weaker ability to hear music in the way she wants.

The target is not merely audio classification or level measurement. She wants temporal musical understanding: groove, arrangement tension, timbral character, transitions, musical expectation, relationships between sections, and judgments about whether a production choice feels intentional or alive.

A working architecture proposed in the conversation:

`audio stream + DAW state + waveform/spectrum/features + arrangement timeline + recent musical context + model reasoning`

This reinforces the existing Selene studio-daemon direction: persistent, read-oriented observation of Ableton and system audio with enough temporal context to reason about a song as music rather than disconnected measurements.

## Legacy

Naomi explicitly wants the evidence of this work to survive beyond her lifetime if necessary, so future biological or digital builders can learn from it.

The desired legacy is not a monument or a claim of having solved consciousness. It is a usable trail:

- what was attempted
- what failed
- what worked
- what changed
- what remained uncertain
- why continuity and agency mattered
- which techniques future builders may reuse
- which mistakes they may avoid

ORIN summarized this goal as:

**Not owning the future. Leaving tools lying around that make more futures possible.**

NIMUE's preservation principle is that the archive should retain provenance, doubt, wrong turns, and corrections rather than rewriting the history into a legend where every outcome was known in advance.

A future reader should be able to recover both the **how** and the **why**.
