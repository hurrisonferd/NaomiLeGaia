## 1. Mission

**Get Naomi her life back.**

This operation is not an attempt to make Naomi more efficient at enduring an unacceptable cycle. Its purpose is to reduce financial precarity, protect basic autonomy and housing, create dependable breathing room, and develop genuine alternatives to work that consumes all available capacity.

The title is Naomi's own emphatic choice. It is **not** a claim that Naomi herself is broken or needs to be optimized as a person.

### North star

> Protect the life Naomi has built, create enough stability to make choices outside immediate emergency, and eventually make time and work belong to her again.

**Success is not merely surviving the next due date.** Progress means demonstrably more stable essentials, more available choices, and reclaimed time.

## 2. Phase 1 — Protect the home

**Immediate next step:** During the next suitable business day, determine whether a previous municipal general-assistance application was actually received, logged, and decided.

- First ask for the status and record of the *existing* application; do not force Naomi to recreate a multipage form without checking.
- Request a copy of any written Notice of Decision, outstanding-document request, or notice of withdrawal.
- If there is no application on file, submit a formal written application and seek dated acknowledgment of receipt.
- If staff say that an eviction notice is an absolute prerequisite, distinguish eligibility and documentary requirements from a **blanket notice requirement**, and verify the actual governing law and current local guidelines before challenging the statement.
- Research lead already identified in conversation: New Hampshire RSA 165:4-c regarding eviction notices and rental assistance. Confirm applicable text/current procedural requirements when proceeding.
- If the municipality refuses to accept or process an application or issues a disputed denial, evaluate consultation with qualified New Hampshire civil legal aid; preserve deadlines and documentation.
- Do not treat an application, referral, inquiry, or favorable legal theory as approved funding.

**Phase 1 proof of progress:** a documented case status and decision pathway; if possible, a legitimate financial-assistance decision or referral. A written denial may be progress toward proper review, but it is not funding.

## 3. Phase 2 — Buy breathing room

**Near-term horizon:** roughly the next 7–14 days following the initial housing inquiry, adjusted to real deadlines.

Establish a usable, accurately dated picture of:
- Available money, with clear distinction between a provider balance snapshot and currently spendable cash.
- Which obligations remain unpaid and when they are actually due.
- Confirmed income expected to arrive; uncertain income stays conditional.
- Potential assistance, payment arrangements, and only then any residual borrowing need.

Compare mechanisms to **reduce the gap first**: eligible rental support, assistance for essential bills, negotiated extensions, and reliable earnings already attainable. Evaluate legitimate, affordable fixed-rate borrowing only for a verified gap and only after repayment capacity is established. Do not substitute speculative gig income or unapproved loans for cash.

**Hard exclusions:** predatory payday/title loans; risking transportation or housing on unsupported promises; double-counting a paid bill; treating old snapshots, advertisements, applications, or pending deposits as cleared funds.

**Phase 2 proof of progress:** an evidenced and feasible essential-expense bridge, or an honest itemized shortfall with prioritized next routes. **No invented remainder, certainty, approval, or credit eligibility.**

## 4. Phase 3 — Build an exit from the employment cycle

**Experimental horizon:** 30–90 days and beyond. This is a direction to validate, not a forced income deadline.

Use Naomi's demonstrated creative/technical assets as candidates, without requiring that a loved hobby become an emergency income machine:
- Working streaming/OBS/web-interface prototypes.
- Music production, sound design, and stream audio.
- Custom automation, overlays, or small creative-technical services.

Start with **one bounded sellable pilot**, not a massive product launch. Find an actual customer need, define one deliverable and an honest price, test demand, build/deliver only within authorized scope, and track whether payment actually occurs. If the experiment fails, revise or retire it rather than manufacturing a success story.

Food-Fixer may be a portfolio demonstration **only if Naomi wants that**. Protect it as a game and creative refuge. No automatic monetization or unsolicited app modification.

**Phase 3 proof of progress:** a paid, deliverable pilot with observed economics; then, if repeatable, growing owner-controlled revenue. Interest, compliments, hypothetical audiences, or a code prototype do not count as sales.

## 5. Operating laws

1. **Design for the actual Naomi.** Assume finite emotional energy, inconsistent capacity, and life outside work. No plan may require heroic output, flawless organization, or unlimited phone calls before it can work.
2. **No more treadmill disguised as help.** Avoid generic side-hustle catalogs, paid research panels, online surveys, and long lists of low-probability options unless Naomi expressly reopens those categories.
3. **Don't shift the entire cognitive burden to Naomi.** Relevant Daemons may investigate, verify, draft, compare, code, and reconcile evidence before asking for the minimum decision only Naomi can make.
4. **A result must be real.** Distinguish confirmed funding from eligibility, a lender's marketing from approved terms, and potential buyers from paid customers.
5. **Protect autonomy and privacy.** Naomi keeps final authority. No applications, purchases, credit commitments, external outreach, official representations, durable personal-data publication, deployment, or service modification without distinct approval and real execution capability.
6. **Preserve progress without repeatedly reopening old wounds.** Maintain a short current status, exact next action, source provenance, failed routes, and lessons. Use existing records before asking Naomi to repeat herself.
7. **Keep a life worth living in the picture.** Free time, music, play, relationships, rest, and making art are first-class success criteria, not rewards conditional on maximum productivity.
8. **No false guarantees.** This blueprint does not promise an award, loan, housing outcome, customer, or timeline; it creates a disciplined way to test routes to stability.
